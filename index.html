QUICK TUTORIAL

Start testing eLOG in five steps

01
Protect this device

Create an 8+ character device password. It locks the app interface; keep the password somewhere safe.

02
Log one complete trade

Open Trade Journal, add the market, entry and exit, size, session, setup checklist, notes, and optional before/after screenshots. eLOG calculates the result.

03
Review and correct it

Use Overview or Trade Journal to edit the entry. Select one or several trades to delete; beta deletions cannot be undone unless you restore a backup.

04
Study your process

Check Analytics, Trading Rules, and Playbook. Add your own rules and setups, then compare adherence and performance by market.

05
Back up before testing hard

In Settings, create an on-device restore point and download a data copy. New Google sign-in and cloud enrollment are locked previews for now.
